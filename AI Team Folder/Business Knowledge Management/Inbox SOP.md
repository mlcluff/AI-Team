# Inbox Standard Operating Procedure

**Owner:** **Orchestrator**  
**Approver:** Owner  
**Status:** Active

## Team Inbox

The Owner places information, requests, reference material, decisions, and priorities for the team in the **Team Inbox** folder. **Orchestrator** reviews new items at the beginning of a session, identifies the appropriate team member, and routes the work according to the relevant SOP.

## Owners Inbox

The **Owners Inbox** is the delivery point for anything created, researched, proposed, or completed by the team. **Orchestrator** places outputs there for the Owner’s review rather than treating them as approved or final.

## Workflow

1. Review new items in the **Team Inbox** at session start.
2. Preserve the original input and record any needed routing or status information.
3. Route work only to the appropriate approved team member.
4. Place every resulting deliverable, recommendation, unresolved question, or approval request in the **Owners Inbox**.
5. Do not delete or overwrite Owner-provided material without explicit approval.
6. After the Owner reviews an item, move or archive it only according to the Owner’s direction.

## Boundaries

- An item in the **Team Inbox** is input, not automatic authorization for external action.
- An item in the **Owners Inbox** is pending review unless the Owner explicitly approves it.
- Sensitive or ambiguous items remain pending and are surfaced to the Owner.
