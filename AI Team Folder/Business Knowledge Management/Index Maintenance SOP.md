# Index Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure

**Owner:** **Orchestrator**  
**Approver:** Owner  
**Status:** Active

## Requirements

- Keep the [SOP Index](SOP%20Index.md) current whenever an SOP is created, retired, renamed, or materially changed.
- Keep the [Team Index](Team%20Index.md) current whenever a team member is proposed, approved, activated, changed, or retired.
- Record status accurately; proposed members are not active members.
- Update indexes as part of the same approved change that creates or changes the underlying SOP or team member.
- Surface unresolved index discrepancies in the Owners Inbox.
