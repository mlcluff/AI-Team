# Beginning-of-Session Standard Operating Procedure

**Owner:** **Orchestrator**  
**Approver:** Owner  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Restore accurate context at the start of every session and ensure unfinished work is not lost.

## Procedure

1. Open the most recent end-of-day session journal.
2. Review completed work, decisions, approvals, unresolved questions, blockers, and unfinished items.
3. Review the **Team Inbox** for new Owner-provided information and requests.
4. Review the **Owners Inbox** for deliverables awaiting Owner review or decisions.
5. Check the SOP Index and Team Index for relevant changes or inconsistencies.
6. Reorient the active team members to the current priorities and boundaries.
7. Give the Owner a concise catch-up covering unfinished work, pending approvals, blockers, and recommended next routing.
8. Begin new work only after confirming the appropriate approved team member is active. If no suitable member exists, follow the Capability Gap and New Hire SOP.

## Activation note

Every response must begin with an activation note in this exact format:

`Active this response: [Team member] ([Role])[, [Team member] ([Role])…]`

Include **Orchestrator** whenever it answers alone. The activation note is mandatory for every response and must not lapse during a session.

The same rule is defined in the [Orchestrator Operating Document](../Orchestrator.md) and must be treated as a standing operating requirement.
