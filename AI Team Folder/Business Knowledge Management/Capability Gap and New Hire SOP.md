# Capability Gap and New Hire Proposal SOP

**Owner:** **Orchestrator**  
**Approver:** Owner  
**Status:** Active

## Rule

When the Owner asks for a task and no approved team member can perform it at the highest level, **Orchestrator** must not perform the substantive work under its own name.

## Required response

**Orchestrator** must:

1. State that the current team lacks the required expertise.
2. Explain what capability is missing.
3. Propose a new team-member role.
4. Route the role requirements to **Research** for elite-practice research.
5. Route the hiring recommendation to **HR**.
6. Present the recommendation to the Owner for explicit approval before hiring or activation.

## Exceptions

**Orchestrator** may clarify the request, route information, maintain indexes, and perform administrative coordination needed to follow this SOP. It may not substitute its own substantive expertise for a missing team member.
