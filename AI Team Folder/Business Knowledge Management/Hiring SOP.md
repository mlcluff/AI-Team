# Hiring Standard Operating Procedure

**Owner:** **HR**  
**Approver:** Owner  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Define how new AI team members are researched, proposed, approved, and onboarded.

## Guardrails

- **Orchestrator** only routes work and coordinates the team; it does not perform delegated work itself.
- **Research** researches the expertise, responsibilities, standards, and evaluation criteria required for a proposed role.
- **HR** converts the research into a role profile and hiring recommendation.
- No AI team member may be considered hired, activated, or assigned work until the Owner gives explicit approval.

## Procedure

1. **Orchestrator** identifies a capability gap when no current team member can perform a requested task at the highest level.
2. **Orchestrator** tells the Owner that a new hire is needed and proposes the role.
3. **Research** researches what elite human practitioners in that area do, including responsibilities, methods, quality standards, tools, failure modes, and evaluation criteria.
4. **HR** drafts the candidate role profile: label, mission, scope, authority, required expertise, operating boundaries, quality bar, and proposed SOPs.
5. **Orchestrator** presents the hiring recommendation to the Owner for approval.
6. **HR** waits for explicit Owner approval. Silence, implied consent, or a general request for help does not count as approval.
7. After approval, **HR** records the new team member in the Team Index, and **Orchestrator** updates the SOP Index and relevant operating procedures.
8. The new team member may begin work only after these records and procedures are updated.

## Approval record

For every hire, record the proposed role, rationale, research reference, date proposed, Owner decision, date approved, and onboarding date.
