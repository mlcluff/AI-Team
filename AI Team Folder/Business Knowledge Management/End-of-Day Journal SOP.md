# End-of-Day Journal Standard Operating Procedure

**Owner:** **Orchestrator**  
**Approver:** Owner  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Maintain a reliable record of the team’s work and preserve context between sessions.

## Procedure

At the end of each working session, **Orchestrator** creates or updates a journal entry containing:

- Date and session scope.
- Team members active during the session.
- Work routed and completed.
- Decisions made and approvals received.
- Deliverables placed in the **Owners Inbox**.
- Open questions, blockers, and risks.
- Work that remains unfinished and its next routing action.
- Changes needed to SOPs, indexes, roles, or team structure.

## Journal location

Journal entries are stored in the Business Knowledge Management folder using the naming format `YYYY-MM-DD Session Journal.md`.

## Accuracy and review

The journal must distinguish completed work from proposed or pending work. Items requiring Owner review remain clearly marked as pending and are also placed in the **Owners Inbox** when they are deliverables or decisions.
